# 🌶️ PepTalk

### Pepper Information, Heat Levels, and Recipes
SpiceSense helps home cooks and pepper enthusiasts explore pepper varieties, compare heat levels, and discover recipes featuring different peppers.

## 👥 WHO — Our Target User
**Target Audience:** Home cooks and pepper enthusiasts who want to learn about different peppers and use them in cooking.

**Specific User:** Our initial focus is on a home cook who wants to explore unfamiliar pepper varieties, understand their heat levels, and find recipes without searching multiple websites.

**User Story:**
- As a home cook or pepper enthusiast,
- I want to explore different peppers, learn about their heat levels and flavors, and find recipes that use them,
- so that I can make informed decisions when cooking.

**Team Members:**
- Poppy Dalton, Elizabeth Shelton, Bella O'rear, and Connor Smith

## 🎯 WHY — Problem and Value
- Information about pepper varieties, flavor profiles, Scoville heat ratings, and recipes is spread across multiple websites. 
- Finding and comparing this information can take time, especially for people who are unfamiliar with different pepper varieties.

**Value Proposition:**
For home cooks and pepper enthusiasts, our application SpiceSense brings pepper information, Scoville heat ratings, and recipes together in one place because it helps users compare pepper varieties and find useful cooking information without searching multiple websites.

### What SpiceSense Provides
- 🌶️ Information about different pepper varieties.
- 🔥 Scoville heat ratings and comparisons.
- 🍳 Recipes featuring specific peppers.
- 📚 Facts about pepper origins, flavors, and culinary uses.

## ✅ DONE — Definition of Success
**At KILO, we should be able to demonstrate that a home cook can:**
- [ ] 🌶️ Find a pepper they want to learn about.
- [ ] 📚 View its facts, flavor profile, and culinary uses.
- [ ] 🖼️ View an image of the pepper.
- [ ] 🔥 View its Scoville heat range and compare it with another pepper.
- [ ] 🍳 Find a recipe featuring that pepper.
- [ ] 🧭 Complete the main tasks without assistance from the development team.

## 🛠️ PLAN — Development Direction

### 🌱 Starting Point
We will research existing resources, define the target user, establish project requirements, and organize our GitHub repository, issues, milestones, and project board.

### Initial Capabilities

#### 🌶️ 1. Pepper Encyclopedia
- Browse different pepper varieties.
- View facts, origins, flavor profiles, and culinary uses.
- Display images with appropriate source attribution.

#### 🔥 2. Scoville Heat Guide
- Display Scoville Heat Unit (SHU) ranges.
- Compare the heat levels of different peppers.
- Use reliable sources and explain that actual heat levels can vary.

#### 🍳 3. Pepper Recipe Finder
- Find recipes featuring selected peppers.
- Display recipe names and useful descriptions.
- Link to original recipe sources.

### ❓ Biggest Unknowns
- [ ] Finding reliable pepper information and Scoville ratings.
- [ ] Determining how to present heat ranges and natural variation.
- [ ] Identifying images that can be used with appropriate permissions and attribution.
- [ ] Deciding how pepper information will be organized and stored.
- [ ] Finding reliable recipe sources.
- [ ] Determining which features are feasible within the project timeline.

### 📅 Project Milestones
- [ ] **Discovery & Planning:** Research existing resources, define users, and establish success criteria.
- [ ] **SpiceSense Core Features:** Develop the pepper encyclopedia, heat guide, and recipe finder.
- [ ] **JULIETT Demo:** Demonstrate the product and gather feedback.
