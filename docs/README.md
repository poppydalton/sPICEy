# 🌶️ PepTalk

### Pepper Information, Heat Levels, and Recipes
PepTalk helps home cooks and pepper enthusiasts explore pepper varieties, compare heat levels, and discover recipes featuring different peppers.

## 👥 WHO — Our Target User
**Target Audience:** Home cooks and pepper enthusiasts who want to learn about different peppers and use them in cooking.

**Specific User:** Our initial focus is on a home cook who wants to explore unfamiliar pepper varieties, understand their heat levels, and find recipes without searching multiple websites.

**User Story:**
- As a home cook or pepper enthusiast,
- I want to explore different peppers, learn about their heat levels and flavors, and find recipes that use them,
- so that I can make informed decisions when cooking.

## 🎯 WHY — Problem and Value
- Information about pepper varieties, flavor profiles, Scoville heat ratings, and recipes is spread across multiple websites. 
- Finding and comparing this information can take time, especially for people who are unfamiliar with different pepper varieties.

**Value Proposition:**
For home cooks and pepper enthusiasts, our application PepTalk brings pepper information, Scoville heat ratings, and recipes together in one place because it helps users compare pepper varieties and find useful cooking information without searching multiple websites.

### What PepTalk Provides
- 🌶️ Information about different pepper varieties.
- 🔥 Scoville heat ratings and comparisons.
- 🍳 Recipes featuring specific peppers.
- 📚 Facts about pepper origins, flavors, and culinary uses.

## 🔎 WHAT ALREADY EXISTS -- landscape
- [LiveEatLearn](https://www.liveeatlearn.com/types-of-pepper/)<br>
   Also intended for home cooks, but suffers from the limitations of being a single blog post. Lacks a lot of relevant information and filled with heavy advertising.
- [KeanyProduce](https://keanyproduce.com/pepper-identification-chart-know-your-peppers/)<br>
   Intended for a general audience, but offloads too much information onto the user. It easily becomes overwhelming when searching for a single pepper's entry. Mainly used to sell the supplier's peppers.
- [TasteOfHome](https://www.tasteofhome.com/article/guide-to-peppers-from-sweet-to-spicy/)<br>
   Another blog with heavy advertising.

### Three Bad Things
- Heavy advertising
- Limited creativity
- Heavy wording

### Exemplary Competitor
- [PepperScale](https://pepperscale.com/)<br>
   Lists a multitude of peppers interactively with sauce ratings, recipes, and facts. Is very encompassing, bogged down by advertisements and a limited recipe count and recipe discoverability.

Alternatively, one can consider a manual approach through, for example, purchasing a cookbook, but such methods come with a higher barrier of entry, cannot be modified, and has constraints such as recipe count.

## ✅ DONE — Definition of Success
**At KILO, we should be able to demonstrate that a home cook can:**
- [ ] 🌶️ Find a pepper they want to learn about.
- [ ] 📚 View its facts, flavor profile, and culinary uses.
- [ ] 🖼️ View an image of the pepper.
- [ ] 🔥 View its Scoville heat range and compare it with another pepper.
- [ ] 🍳 Find a recipe featuring that pepper.
- [ ] 🧭 Complete the main tasks without assistance from the development team.

## 🛠️ PLAN — Project System

### 🌱 Starting Point
We are going to utilize Isabella's class project [BookNook](https://github.com/isabellaorear31-design/fullstack-booknook) as a basis for this since it has similar baseline features.

### Direction
We hope the project becomes something comparable to PepperScale without the downsides of its heavy advertising and limited recipe collection.

### Initial Capabilities

#### 🌶️ 1. Pepper Encyclopedia
- Browse different pepper varieties.
- View facts, origins, flavor profiles, and culinary uses.
- Display images with appropriate source attribution.

#### 🔥 2. Scoville Heat Guide
- Display Scoville Heat Unit (SHU) ranges.
- Compare the heat levels of different peppers.
- Use reliable sources and explain that actual heat levels can vary.

#### 🍳 3. Pepper Recipe Finder
- Find recipes featuring selected peppers.
- Display recipe names and useful descriptions.
- Link to original recipe sources.

### ❓ Biggest Unknowns
